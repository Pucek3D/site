# Privacy Notice — ADelier Commerce
## 1. Controller and contact details
The controller of personal data processed in connection with https://adeliercommerce.com is **Kornelia Mazurczyk-Wolska**, an individual operating the website under the **ADelier Commerce** brand, at ul. Daniszewska 30/12, 03-230 Warsaw, Poland. For privacy-related requests, please contact **contact@deliercommerce.com**.

## 2. Data we process
When you submit an inquiry or contact us, we may process your name, email address, company name, inquiry category, preferred language and message, as well as your role and website address if you choose to provide them. We may also receive other information you voluntarily include in correspondence.

The website is hosted by VH.PL, which provides the technical infrastructure necessary to operate the website. The hosting provider may process technical data, including IP addresses, access timestamps and server logs, for website operation, maintenance and security. Technical-log retention is limited to website operation and security needs and depends on the hosting configuration.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases
We use inquiry data to respond, discuss potential services and manage related correspondence. Where you request steps prior to entering into a contract, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. For general inquiries and routine correspondence, the legal basis may be our legitimate interest in managing communications under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. We do not use your inquiry to send newsletters or advertising without a separate applicable legal basis and any required consent.

Technical data may be processed where necessary to operate, protect and troubleshoot the website, on the basis of our legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, subject to applicable law.

## 4. Providing information
Providing information is voluntary. However, the form's required fields are necessary to submit an inquiry and allow us to respond. Additional fields are optional. Please do not include sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely needed for your inquiry.

## 5. Retention
If an inquiry does not lead to cooperation, its correspondence data will be retained **for no longer than 60 days after the correspondence ends**. Different periods may apply where cooperation begins or a separate legal obligation or a justified need to establish, pursue or defend legal claims applies.

Backups and technical logs may follow separate cycles determined by the hosting setup. Messages and attachments will be deleted from the active mailbox under the retention procedure. Any backup copies containing these data may be deleted on a separate, limited backup rotation cycle.

## 6. Recipients and service providers
We use the following service providers, who process data on our behalf only to the extent necessary for their service:

- **VH.PL** hosts the website, as described in section 2, and provides the email mailbox contact@deliercommerce.com, processing the correspondence received there.

The service used to forward form submissions to the mailbox will be named here before the form is put into operation.

## 7. Transfers outside the European Economic Area
We do not currently plan to transfer personal data outside the European Economic Area. If a service provider engaged later, for example to forward form submissions, processes data outside the EEA, this section will be updated before that happens to name the provider and the safeguards used. You can request information by contacting **contact@deliercommerce.com**.

## 8. Analytics, cookies and newsletter
We currently do not use analytics tools or advertising pixels and do not offer newsletter subscriptions. We do not use cookies or similar technologies for analytics or advertising purposes. Website operation and security may involve technical information processed by our hosting provider, including the server logs described in this notice. Before introducing any technology that requires prior consent, we will implement an appropriate consent mechanism.

No newsletter is currently offered. Should this change, separate subscription and unsubscribe rules, along with any required notices and consents, will be provided.

## 9. Automated decision-making
We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects for you.

## 10. Your rights
Where applicable under the GDPR, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing or data portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact **contact@deliercommerce.com** to exercise your rights. You may also lodge a complaint with the **President of the Polish Personal Data Protection Office (UODO)** (www.uodo.gov.pl).

If you are in the United Kingdom, you have corresponding rights under the UK GDPR and may also lodge a complaint with the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)** (www.ico.org.uk).

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

## 11. Updates
This notice will be updated if processing practices, hosting, analytics, newsletter services or the operator's legal status change.
